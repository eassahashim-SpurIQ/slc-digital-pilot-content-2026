## Priority Signal Direction Agreed

- Phase 1: companies using text/SMS codes for wallet/authentication flows; poor App Store reviews about account takeover/missing money/unauthorized transactions; newly licensed or registered wallet/crypto firms; recent hires in identity/fraud/security/compliance.
- Phase 2: product launches; events/G2/vendor stack/funding/journals; regions where text codes are being phased out; regulator movement against text codes; breach/fraud/SIM-swap stories.
