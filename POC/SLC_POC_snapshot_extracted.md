## Positioning

Signal-led outbound for SLC Digital.

Core premise: SLC's category is new, so prospects are not actively searching for it yet. SpurIQ identifies buyers when they show fraud/authentication pain signals, reaches them with signal-specific messaging, and hands warm replies to SLC.

## Key Claims

- List-based cold email usually gets under 1% reply rate and often lands in spam.
- Signal-triggered emails can produce 6-14% reply rates.
- SpurIQ uses 80+ data and signal tools in a waterfall, with contacts multi-verified.
- Day 10 is the target for first full-capacity send after domains are bought, warmed, and messaging is agreed.

## Market Segments

1. Wallets and payment providers
   - Motion: Email-first
   - Rationale: Highest account-takeover pain and fastest cycle; described as the pilot's engine room.

2. Enterprise workforce and privileged access
   - Motion: Email-first
   - Rationale: CISOs/CIOs replacing OTPs for employee and admin authentication.

3. Banks and digital banks
   - Motion: Email + LinkedIn
   - Rationale: Longer poll; seeded during pilot and harvested over quarters.

4. Evidence-grade attestation
   - Motion: New use case
   - Rationale: Body-cam and media provenance buyers.

5. Telcos and carriers
   - Motion: LinkedIn only
   - Rationale: Infrastructure partners; exec-to-exec motion, not cold email.

## Workflow

1. Build the market.
   - SpurIQ maps SLC's addressable universe.
   - Contacts are verified through waterfall enrichment.
   - Segments are tiered by the motion they deserve.

2. Catch the buying moment.
   - Agents scan LinkedIn, hiring boards, news, and security publications.
   - Prospects enter sequences only when a live signal indicates market timing.

3. Convert the signal.
   - Emails reference the specific trigger signal.
   - Multi-touch sequences use low-effort CTAs to earn a second response.
   - Replies are sorted and handed to SLC's sales engineers.

## Live Market Signals

- Hiring fraud / identity / trust-and-safety roles
- Account takeover or SIM-swap incident in the news
- New app or wallet launch
- OTP fatigue and account-recovery complaints
- Security architecture chatter from executives
- Fresh funding
- New CISO / CRO in seat
- Publications supplied by SLC can be added to the scraping/signals list

## LinkedIn Layer

1. Engager capture
   - Agents monitor company page and executive posts.
   - People who react or comment are identified.
   - ICP-matched engagers can be reached on SLC's behalf.

2. Human pace and account safety
   - Runs from one or two senior executive profiles.
   - Capped at 15-20 adds per day.
   - Replies only to people already engaging.

3. Telco track
   - Telco partnerships are treated as executive relationship motions.
   - CTOs are tiered and steadily added from SLC executive profiles.

## Pilot Scope

- Monthly volume: 2,000-3,000 signal-qualified contacts.
- Email volume: 6,000-12,000 multi-touch emails.
- Approval: Every draft approved by SLC before sending.
- Ramp: Day 10 for domains registered and warmed, with messaging iterated together.
- Term: 2-3 month paid pilot.
- Price: $2,000-$2,500 per month.
- Included: Data, domains, copy, sending, and replies.

## Deliverability / Domain Safety

- SLC's primary domain is not touched.
- Campaigns run on parallel sender domains registered, warmed, and monitored by SpurIQ.
- The goal is to protect SLC's primary domain reputation and deliverability.

## Proof Point

SpurIQ cites prior work for security vendors, including Sectona in privileged access management, selling into the Middle East and Europe and expanding into ANZ using this motion.

## Iteration Model

Weekly iteration is part of the offer. Positioning and learnings from customer calls feed into the following week's messaging and campaign angles.
